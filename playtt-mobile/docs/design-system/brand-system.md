# PlayTT Brand System

## Brand Essence
- PlayTT is premium, private, calm, and high-tech.
- The product should feel confident and effortless, not loud or gamified.
- The core promise is simple: book a private table tennis session with minimal friction and clear control.

## Positioning
- Category: autonomous private sports booking.
- Audience: students, young professionals, casual players, and small groups who want a polished self-serve experience.
- Experience goal: the first-time visitor should understand the product within one viewport and trust it within one flow.

## Core Identity Locks
- Name: `PlayTT`
- Tagline: `Book a pod. Play on.`
- Product rule: dark mode only.
- Accent rule: electric azure is the hero brand color and must remain the primary call-to-action signal.
- Tone rule: premium calm, concise, low hype, action-first.

## Color System
- Primary: `#00b7ff`
- Primary foreground: `#041019`
- Background: `#07111d`
- Elevated background: `#0b1627`
- Card: `#101b2b`
- Border: `#203149`
- Muted text: `#92a6bf`
- Destructive: `#ff3b30`
- Success: `#00ff66`
- Warning: `#ffb800`

## Typography
- Primary UI and heading family: `Space Grotesk`
- Monospace and numeric utility family: `Geist Mono`
- Heading style: bold, tight tracking, short line lengths
- Body style: medium contrast, calm spacing, no dense paragraphs
- Number treatment: use mono or stable tabular presentation for times, totals, and scores when alignment matters

## Voice and Writing
- Keep labels short and literal.
- Prefer calm confidence over sporty hype.
- Prefer action verbs: `Book now`, `Create account`, `Verify email`, `Reserve booking`.
- Avoid filler copy, shouting, and motivational language.
- Explain only the next step unless more context materially reduces confusion.

## Visual Language
- Surfaces should feel layered, glassy, and intentional without becoming flashy.
- Use gradients and glow sparingly, mainly to support hierarchy and focus.
- Preserve generous radius, soft shadows, and clear panel separation.
- Avoid clutter, over-decoration, or too many competing accents.

## Accessibility Principles
- Contrast must remain strong on dark surfaces.
- Focus states must be visible and use the primary ring color.
- Motion must be subtle and respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Important actions should remain clear without relying only on color.

## Logo and Mark Usage
- Primary mark: circular `TT` monogram paired with `PLAYTT`.
- Use the mark at the start of marketing, auth, and product shells.
- Keep the mark on dark surfaces with azure emphasis.
- Do not introduce alternate brand colors or light-mode variants.
