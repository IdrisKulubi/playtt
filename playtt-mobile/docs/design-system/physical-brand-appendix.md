# PlayTT Physical Brand Appendix

## Intent
This appendix keeps the digital product and the physical pod experience aligned. It is not a full interior-design playbook. It defines the minimum brand rules needed so signage, hardware, and pod atmosphere feel like the same product as the app.

## Storefront
- Keep visibility high so passersby can understand the experience quickly.
- Use clear glass wherever privacy rules allow.
- The sign should pair crisp white with electric azure emphasis on `TT`.
- Avoid cluttered promotional graphics that conflict with the calm premium tone.

## Pod Interior
- Use matte dark surfaces so the table and lighting read clearly.
- Keep the space clean, controlled, and intentional rather than decorative.
- Ambient azure light should support mood, not overpower the room.
- Functional white lighting over the table always takes priority over dramatic effects.

## Hardware Presentation
- Smart locks, cameras, tablets, and other mounted devices should be matte dark where possible.
- Hardware should look integrated into the environment, not bolted on as an afterthought.
- Cables and mounting details should be hidden or minimized.

## Wayfinding
- If floor or wall directional cues are used, they should be minimal and use the core accent color.
- Wayfinding should help first-time users move from entrance to table naturally without adding visual noise.

## On-site Screen Behavior
- Any tablet or control screen inside the pod should follow the same dark-only PlayTT UI system.
- Large action zones, strong contrast, and reduced visual clutter matter more than density.

## Consistency Rules
- Do not introduce alternate accent colors for a single location.
- Do not switch to a bright or playful retail aesthetic in physical spaces.
- The physical environment should feel like an extension of the digital product: private, controlled, premium, and easy to understand.
