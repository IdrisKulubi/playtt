# PlayTT Product Context

## Register
product

## Product Purpose
PlayTT is a premium, self-serve booking product for private table tennis sessions. The product should help a player move from discovery to reservation with very little friction, while keeping the experience calm, clear, and trustworthy.

## Users
- Students and young professionals booking a private pod with friends
- Returning players who want a fast repeat booking path
- First-time visitors who need to understand the offer within one viewport

## Core Experience
The product is built around one primary journey:
1. Understand what PlayTT is
2. Pick a location
3. See open timing availability
4. Set group size
5. Review a clear checkout summary
6. Complete payment and access later phases

Every major screen should support that journey. If a screen does not help the player move toward a booking, it should be quiet and secondary.

## Brand Character
- Premium, private, calm, high-tech
- Confident, never flashy
- Distinctly blue-led
- Clean enough to feel trustworthy, warm enough to feel human

## Tone
- Concise and action-first
- Clear labels over clever copy
- Calm confidence over sports hype
- Never robotic, generic, or startup-pitchy

## Strategic Principles
- Start with the player’s real decision, not the system’s internal structure
- Show less, but make each step more legible
- Use strong hierarchy so the next action is always obvious
- Preserve trust through pricing clarity, stable layout, and consistent controls
- Keep mobile behavior first-class, not compressed desktop

## Design Intent
PlayTT should not look like a generic AI-generated dashboard or SaaS landing page. Avoid repeated feature-card grids, decorative glass everywhere, and over-explaining every section. The interface should feel intentional, structured, and premium, with a disciplined use of electric blue, darker ink-toned neutrals, and sharper editorial rhythm.

## Anti-References
- Generic startup landing pages with evenly spaced hero, metrics, and feature cards
- Overused glassmorphism and floating gradient blobs
- Sports apps that feel noisy, gamified, or neon-heavy
- Minimalist UIs that feel empty rather than precise

## Success Standard
A first-time visitor should feel that PlayTT is a real premium product within a few seconds, and a player entering the booking flow should feel guided by the interface rather than asked to figure it out.
