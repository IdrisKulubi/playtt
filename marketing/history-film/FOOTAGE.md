## Licences: what they mean for an ad

- **Public domain / CC0**: free to use, no conditions. Best choice.
- **CC BY / CC BY 2.0**: free, but the credits must name the author.
- **CC BY-SA** (most Dutch newsreels, African Games photos): free with credit, **but the finished video must also be released as CC BY-SA**, so anyone could reuse it with credit. That's fine for a promo if you accept it; otherwise we use only the public-domain clips and stills.
- **Pexels / Mixkit**: free for commercial use, no credit needed. Don't resell the raw clips.
- Not usable: Olympic, WTT, ITTF or TV broadcast footage.
- C16 (2024 China News clip, CC BY 3.0) is modern news. Any 1971 archive shots inside it belong to someone else, so leave it out.
